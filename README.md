# Stock-Protfolio-Optimization-
This project focuses on optimizing the allocation of a SAR 100,000 investment portfolio across selected companies listed in the Saudi stock market.

The objective was to identify the optimal investment strategy that maximizes expected returns while considering investment constraints and risk factors through Operations Research techniques.

Problem Statement

Investors often face the challenge of allocating capital across multiple investment opportunities while balancing profitability and risk.

This project aims to determine the optimal distribution of funds among selected Saudi stocks using mathematical optimization techniques.

Dataset
Historical stock price data from the Saudi Stock Market (TASI).
Selected companies from different sectors.
Data used for educational and academic purposes only.

Results
Initial average expected returns across the studied stocks were approximately -5%.
The optimization model generated a portfolio with an expected return of approximately 11%.
The model allocated a significant portion of investments to Al Rajhi Bank and Saudi National Bank (SNB) based on the optimization criteria.
The solution also suggested retaining a portion of the capital to satisfy the imposed constraints and risk considerations.
